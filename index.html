3. Permissions

The app uses internet access, network state and (for ad services) the advertising ID permission. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

4. Advertising and your choice

The app shows ads (banner, interstitial and rewarded). On first launch you are asked whether you accept personalized ads; the ad service starts only after you choose. If you accept, ads may be tailored to your interests; if you decline, non-personalized (generic) ads are shown, and the number of ads does not change. You can change your choice anytime in Settings → General. You can also limit personalization or reset your advertising ID in your device’s Settings → Google → Ads.

6. International transfers

Ad partners and the hosting provider may process data in countries outside Türkiye (e.g. USA, EU). This transfer is needed for the related features (ads, multiplayer, leaderboards); you can limit ad personalization as described above.

7. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app. Your nickname and leaderboard records are stored in a database on our server and are automatically deleted after 365 days of inactivity. You can also delete them in the app via Settings → General → “Delete my data”; this also resets your device ID and stats. If you cannot connect to the server, or for data held by ad partners, email aykutsen1987@gmail.com.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you notice such a case, email us and we will delete the data.

9. Security

Server connections are encrypted (https/wss) and reasonable technical and organizational measures are applied; however, no internet transmission is completely risk-free. In case of a data breach, the competent authority and, where required, affected users are notified within legal deadlines. Your nickname is visible to other players; we recommend not using your real name.

10. Your rights

You may request access, correction and deletion, object to processing, and withdraw consent where applicable (Turkish KVKK, EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA). Send requests to aykutsen1987@gmail.com. You may also complain to your data protection authority.
